Título: New Year {ciclo}

A page to follow the arrival of the New Year and Christmas around the world, time zone by time zone.

It shows a live countdown and, for each of the 37 time zones, builds a greeting ready to post on social media, with the flags and cities celebrating at that moment: from UTC+14 in Kiribati to UTC−12.

- Live countdown.
- New Year and Christmas tabs, with a button to copy each greeting.
- More than 300 cities, named in the local language and in English.
- Editable greeting; the year updates itself.
- A tab with the New Year of other calendars: Chinese, Jewish, Islamic, Persian, Ethiopian and Thai.
